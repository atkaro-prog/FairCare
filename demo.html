Hospital-affiliated offices can sometimes bill differently from independent offices. FairCare helps you ask about potential facility fees before your appointment.

02 / YOUR PRE-APPOINTMENT GUIDE

Ask the billing office

  1. Does this location charge a separate hospital facility fee?
  2. Which entity will bill me, and what are its NPI and place-of-service code?
  3. What are the CPT/HCPCS codes and separately billed professional services?
  4. Is every billing entity in-network with my specific insurance plan?
  5. Can I receive a complete written estimate?
